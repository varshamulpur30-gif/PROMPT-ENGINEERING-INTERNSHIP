# TASK 02 — PROMPTING FOR CREATIVITY

## Creative Task: Eco-Friendly Water Bottle Advertisement

## Variant 1 – Basic Prompt

**Prompt:**

Write an advertisement for an eco-friendly water bottle.

### Output

**GO Green, GO Fresh! 🌿💧**

### Eco-Friendly Water Bottle

Stay hydrated while protecting our planet! 🌎

Our eco-friendly water bottle is reusable, durable, lightweight, and made from sustainable materials. Say goodbye to single-use plastic bottles and make a greener choice every day.

🌱 Eco-Friendly & Reusable  
💧 Leak-Proof & Durable  
🎒 Lightweight & Easy to Carry  
♻️ Reduces Plastic Waste

**Drink Smart. Live Green. Save Earth!**

Choose the bottle that cares for you and the planet.

**Go Green — One Sip at a Time! 🌿**

## Variant 2 – Youthful Few-Shot Prompt

**Prompt:**

Create an advertisement for an eco-friendly water bottle. Use the following examples to guide the style and tone:

**Example 1:**

“Fresh taste. Fresh feeling. Fresh planet. Choose reusable, choose better.”

**Example 2:**

“Small change. Big impact. Carry a bottle that helps reduce plastic waste.”

Now write a new advertisement for the eco-friendly water bottle. Make it catchy, positive, youthful, and suitable for college students. Include a short slogan and a clear call to action. Keep it under 100 words.

### Output

**Eco-Friendly Water Bottle Advertisement**

**Sip Smart. Live Green. 🌱💧**

Fresh water. Fresh vibes. Cleaner planet. 🌎

Meet the bottle made for your everyday college life—reusable, stylish, lightweight, and eco-friendly.

Skip single-use plastic and make every refill count. Whether you’re heading to class, the gym, or hanging out with friends, carry a bottle that keeps you hydrated while helping the planet.

**One Bottle. Less Plastic. More Impact.**

👉 **Refill. Reuse. Repeat. Choose yours today!**

---

## Variant 3 – Premium Few-Shot Prompt

**Prompt:**

Create an advertisement for an eco-friendly water bottle in a premium, emotional, and elegant brand style.

**Example 1:**

“Carry a little luxury. Leave a little less behind.”

**Example 2:**

“Designed for your everyday moments, made with a thought for tomorrow.”

Now write a new advertisement for the eco-friendly water bottle. Target environmentally conscious young adults. Make the tone sophisticated, emotional, and inspiring. Include a memorable headline, a short body, and a strong call to action. Keep it under 100 words.

### Output

**Elegance That Leaves Less Behind. 🌿**

Some choices are small. Their impact doesn’t have to be.

Meet the eco-friendly water bottle—thoughtfully designed for modern living, beautifully made for everyday moments. Reusable, refined, and ready to go wherever life takes you.

Because caring for the planet can feel as effortless as carrying something you love.

**Sip with purpose. Carry the change.**

**Choose beautifully. Live consciously. Start with every refill.**

---

## Comparison & Analysis

### Variant 1 – Basic Prompt

The basic prompt produced a straightforward advertisement that focused on the bottle's features such as being reusable, durable, lightweight, and eco-friendly.

### Variant 2 – Youthful Few-Shot Prompt

The few-shot prompt produced a more catchy and engaging advertisement. The examples helped guide the writing style, while the college-student audience made the content more youthful and relatable.

### Variant 3 – Premium Few-Shot Prompt

The premium few-shot prompt produced a sophisticated and emotional advertisement. It used elegant language and focused more on lifestyle, purpose, and environmental consciousness.

### Overall Analysis

The results show that prompt structure, examples, tone, and audience instructions influence the AI's creative output. The basic prompt generated a general advertisement, while the few-shot prompts created more distinctive styles. The examples helped guide the model toward the desired tone and persona.

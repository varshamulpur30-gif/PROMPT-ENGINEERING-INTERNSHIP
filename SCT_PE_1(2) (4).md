# TASK 01 – WRITING BETTER PROMPTS

## Prompt 1 – Vague Prompt

“Explain artificial intelligence.”

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the branch of computer science that focuses on creating machines and software that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

These tasks include:

- **Learning** from data and experience.
- **Reasoning** to solve problems.
- **Understanding language** (such as speech and text).
- **Recognizing images and objects**.
- **Making decisions** based on available information.

## Types of AI

1. **Narrow AI (Weak AI):**
   - Designed to perform a specific task.
   - Examples: Voice assistants, recommendation systems, spam filters.
2. **General AI (Strong AI):**
   - A theoretical form of AI that can perform any intellectual task a human can do.
   - It does not yet exist.

## Applications of AI

- Healthcare (disease diagnosis, medical imaging)
- Education (personalized learning)
- Banking (fraud detection)
- Transportation (self-driving vehicles)
- Agriculture (crop monitoring)
- Customer service (chatbots)

## Advantages

- Performs repetitive tasks quickly and accurately.
- Works continuously without fatigue.
- Improves decision-making using data.
- Increases productivity and efficiency.

## Disadvantages

- High development and maintenance costs.
- May replace some jobs through automation.
- Can produce biased results if trained on biased data.
- Raises privacy and ethical concerns.

## Prompt 1 – Improved Prompt

“Explain Artificial Intelligence (AI) in simple language for a college student who is new to AI. Give 5 key points, 3 real-world examples, and a short conclusion. Keep the explanation under 200 words.”

## Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that enables computers and machines to **learn, understand, solve problems, and make decisions** similar to human intelligence.

## 5 Key Points

1. **Learning:** AI learns patterns from data and improves its performance.
2. **Problem-solving:** It can analyze information and find solutions.
3. **Decision-making:** AI uses data to make predictions or recommendations.
4. **Language understanding:** It can understand and generate human language.
5. **Automation:** AI can perform repetitive tasks with little human effort.

## 3 Real-World Examples

- **Chatbots:** ChatGPT can understand questions and generate answers.
- **Recommendation systems:** YouTube and Netflix suggest videos or movies based on your interests.
- **Face recognition:** Smartphones use AI to recognize faces and unlock devices.

## Comparison & Analysis

### Comparison

The response to the vague prompt was more detailed and broad, covering types, applications, advantages, and disadvantages of AI.

The improved prompt produced a more focused and organized response. It followed specific instructions by providing 5 key points, 3 real-world examples, and a conclusion. The language was simple and appropriate for a college student who is new to AI.

### Analysis

Adding specific instructions about the audience, structure, and length made the AI response more focused and useful.

## Prompt 3: Vague Prompt

## Write about social media.

### Social Media

Social media refers to online platforms where people can communicate, share information, create content, and connect with others. Examples include Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp, Facebook, and LinkedIn.

## 5 Key Points

1. **Communication:** Helps people stay connected with friends, family, and communities.
2. **Education:** Students can access tutorials, courses, and useful information.
3. **Entertainment:** Provides videos, music, games, and other content.
4. **Career opportunities:** Platforms like LinkedIn help students build professional networks.
5. **Information sharing:** News and ideas can spread quickly through social media.

## Advantages

- Easy communication
- Access to knowledge
- Networking opportunities
- Platform for creativity

## Disadvantages

- Can waste time
- Misinformation can spread quickly
- Privacy and online-safety concerns
- Excessive use can affect daily routines

## Prompt 3 – Improved Prompt

“Explain social media in simple language for a college student. Give 5 important benefits, 5 common uses, and 3 tips for using social media responsibly. Keep the explanation under 200 words.”

## Social Media

Social media is a group of online platforms where people can **communicate, share content, learn, and connect with others**. Examples include Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp, and LinkedIn.

## 5 Important Benefits

1. **Easy communication** with friends, family, and communities.
2. **Learning opportunities** through educational videos and resources.
3. **Career networking** with professionals and organizations.
4. **Information sharing** about news, events, and ideas.
5. **Creativity and expression** through posts, videos, photos, and other content.

## 5 Common Uses

- Chatting and video calling
- Watching educational or entertaining content
- Sharing photos and videos
- Promoting businesses and products
- Finding jobs and professional connections

## 3 Responsible-Use Tips

1. **Manage your time** and avoid excessive scrolling.
2. **Protect your privacy** by avoiding sharing sensitive personal information.
3. **Check information carefully** before believing or sharing posts.

## Compare Prompt 3

### Comparison

The vague prompt produced a general explanation of social media, including its key points, advantages, and disadvantages.

The improved prompt gave a more focused response with exactly 5 benefits, 5 common uses, and 3 responsible-use tips. It also used simple language suitable for a college student.

### Analysis

The improved prompt provided clear instructions about the audience, required information, structure, and length. As a result, the AI response was more organized, specific, and relevant.
